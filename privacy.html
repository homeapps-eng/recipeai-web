Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

The short version

  • RecipeAI is made by Haykaz Sayadyan, an independent app developer in California, USA. We don't sell your personal information.
  • To create recipes and estimate calories, the photos, requests and food preferences you give the app are processed by Google's Gemini AI service.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob. They are personalized only as your device settings and choices allow. Premium has no ads.
  • You can delete your account, and everything we keep about you, from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the RecipeAI app ("RecipeAI Plus" on the App Store, "RecipeAI" on Google Play) and the recipe-ai.io website. RecipeAI is made by Haykaz Sayadyan ("we", "us", "our"), who decides how your information is used. Contact details are at the end of this page.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Your account

  • Sign-in: your email address and name, and how you sign in (email and password, Google, or Sign in with Apple). With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email address; we then receive a private relay address from Apple.
  • Password: handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it.
  • Profile photo: optional. If you add one, we store it.
  • Guest use: if you use RecipeAI without signing up, we create a guest account linked to a random ID for your device, so free limits apply and your recipes are kept. It is stored like a regular account, and you can turn it into one later.

2.2 Your food preferences

Diets, allergies, cuisines and other food preferences you choose, plus settings such as measurement units, language and notification choices. Diets and allergies can say something about your health. We use them only to tailor recipes and to avoid suggesting food you can't eat, never for advertising.

2.3 What you ask the AI, and what it creates

  • Photos of food or ingredients you take or choose. They are sent to the AI service to be analyzed. We don't store them.
  • Typed or spoken requests. When you speak, your phone's own speech recognition (Apple's on iPhone, Google's on Android) turns your voice into text. Your audio is handled by Apple or Google under their terms; only the text reaches us.
  • Results: the recipes and calorie estimates created for you, the recipes you save as favorites, a history of what you created (which recipe, and whether it came from a photo or text), and how many requests you have used, for free limits.
  • Recipes created from typed or spoken requests may be kept together with the request text, without any link to you, so the same request can be answered faster.

2.4 Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play. We receive your subscription status, the plan, its dates and the store's purchase identifiers. We never receive your payment details.

2.5 Device, advertising and usage data

  • Ads (free users): Google AdMob collects your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking), your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.
  • App statistics (iPhone app only): Google Analytics for Firebase collects how the app is used and device information, and the advertising identifier if you allow tracking. The Android app has no analytics tool.
  • Server logs: our servers log requests, including your user ID and IP address, to keep the service running and secure.

2.6 When you contact us

If you email us, use the in-app support form, or report AI-generated content, we receive your email address, your message, and for reports your user ID and app version.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account and create, save and show your recipes and calorie estimates.
  • To tailor recipes to your preferences, diets and allergies.
  • To apply free limits and give Premium subscribers their benefits.
  • To show ads to free users, and to measure them.
  • To answer support requests and review reported content.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • On iPhone, to understand how the app is used and improve it.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don't use your information to train AI models, and we don't sell it.

4. AI processing

Recipes, photo analysis and calorie estimates are created by Google Gemini, through Google's paid Gemini API. To answer a request, it receives your photo or the text of your request, together with your food preferences, including diets and allergies.

  • Under Google's terms for the paid Gemini API, Google doesn't use this data to improve its products. Google may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse, as its terms describe.
  • Pictures of dishes are created by AI from the recipe's name and description. They contain no personal information.
  • On iPhone, the app asks your permission before the first time it sends anything to the AI. You can withdraw it under Settings › Privacy › Use AI features.
  • Please don't include other people, or personal details, in your photos or requests.

AI-generated recipes and calorie estimates can be wrong. Always check ingredients for allergens yourself. See our Terms of Service.

5. Advertising and tracking

  • On iPhone, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking when the app asks. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are shown. You can change your answer in the app's Settings under Privacy choices.
  • Premium removes ads, and the ad-related collection that comes with them.

Some US state laws, such as California's, may treat letting Google use your advertising identifier for personalized ads as "sharing" personal information or as "targeted advertising". We don't sell personal information for money. You can opt out at any time with the settings above.

Do Not Track. We don't respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals. The app uses the device settings and choices described above instead.

8. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and Google Cloud encrypts the data it stores for us. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication, and only the developer can access our systems. No service is completely secure, but we keep what we collect to what RecipeAI needs.

9. Your choices and rights

  • In the app: edit your profile and food preferences; delete your account (Android: Profile › Settings › Remove Account; iPhone: Profile › Settings › Delete Account); manage ad choices as described in section 5; on iPhone, turn AI features off.
  • By email: ask for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, at support@recipe-ai.io, from the email address of your account. We answer within 30 days. See also our account deletion page.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your information, to object to or restrict how it's used, to withdraw consent, to opt out of targeted advertising, and to complain to your data protection authority. We won't treat you differently for using them.

For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: we use your information to provide the service you signed up for (contract), with your consent (personalized ads), and for our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and improving it.

10. Children

RecipeAI is for people aged 13 and older, and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13; if we learn that we have, we delete it. If you believe a child has used RecipeAI, email us. Users aged 13 to 17 should use it with a parent's or guardian's involvement.

11. Where your information is processed

We are in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States. Our service providers may process it in other countries. Where the law requires safeguards for these transfers, we rely on our providers' data protection terms, such as Google's, which include standard contractual clauses.

12. This website

The recipe-ai.io website uses Google Ads conversion tracking, which sets cookies to measure how our ads perform. You can block cookies in your browser and manage Google ads at adssettings.google.com. Our hosting provider may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how RecipeAI handles information, we'll update this page and its effective date. We'll tell you about significant changes in the app or by email before they take effect, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
