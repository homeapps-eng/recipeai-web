How RecipeAI works

  1. Snap it, or say it. Take a photo of what's in your fridge, a dish you loved at a restaurant, or just tell RecipeAI what you're craving.
  2. Get recipes in seconds. Ingredients, step-by-step instructions and a picture of the finished dish, matched to your diet, allergies and preferences. Ask for more variations of the same photo.
  3. Know what's on your plate. Photograph any meal and tap Calculate Calories to get total calories, protein, carbs and fat, plus fiber, sugar and sodium, ingredient by ingredient.

Questions people ask

Can RecipeAI make a recipe from a photo of my ingredients?

Yes. Photograph whatever you have, even three ingredients, and RecipeAI suggests several dishes you can cook with them, each with full instructions.

How does the calorie count from a photo work?

RecipeAI identifies each ingredient in the picture, estimates its portion size and adds up calories and macros. It's an estimate based on what's visible, so it's great for everyday tracking rather than medical use. You can flag any result that looks wrong.

Which languages does RecipeAI speak?

Fourteen, including English, Spanish, French, Italian, Greek, Turkish, Armenian and Chinese. Pick one in Settings and your feed, ingredients and cooking steps all switch instantly.

Is RecipeAI free?

RecipeAI is free to download and use. RecipeAI Premium ($2.99 a month or $29.99 a year) unlocks unlimited generations, enhanced images and priority support.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Both. Get it on the App Store or Google Play. Your cookbook syncs when you sign in.
