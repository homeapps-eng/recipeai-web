Support

Help with the RecipeAI app for iPhone and Android

Contact us

The quickest way is from inside the app: open Profile → Help & Support, type your message and send it. Your account details come along, so we can look into it right away.

You can also email us. We read every message and usually reply within two business days.

Email support@recipe-ai.io

Helpful to include: your device and app version (Profile → Settings), what you tapped, and what happened instead. For a recipe or calorie result that looks wrong, the flag button on that screen sends us the exact result.

Common questions

How do I get recipes from a photo?

Tap the camera button on the home screen, photograph your ingredients or a dish, and tap Generate Recipes. You can also tap the microphone and simply say what you're craving. The first time, the app asks your permission to send the photo or request to the AI service.

How accurate is the calorie estimate?

RecipeAI identifies the ingredients it can see, estimates their portions and adds up calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar and sodium. It is an estimate from a photo, good for everyday tracking, not for medical decisions. Hidden ingredients such as oil or sauce can be missed. If a result looks wrong, tap the flag button to report it.

Can I change the language?

Yes. Open Profile → Settings → Language and pick one of the fourteen languages. Your feed, ingredients and cooking steps switch immediately.

Where are my saved recipes?

Tap the heart on any recipe to save it. Saved recipes appear in Favorites, grouped by the day you saved them. When you're signed in, they sync to your account so they follow you to a new phone.

What does Premium include, and how do I cancel?

RecipeAI Premium removes the daily limits of the free plan and unlocks unlimited recipe generations and enhanced images. It is billed by Apple or Google, so you manage or cancel it there:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RecipeAI.
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → RecipeAI.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; Premium stays active until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

I paid but Premium isn't active

Open Profile → Premium and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID or Google account that paid. If it still doesn't work, send us the receipt date from your Apple or Google purchase history.

How do I delete my account?

Inside the app, open Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account and everything we keep about you. The steps are also on our account deletion page.

Something else?

Email support@recipe-ai.io. Feature ideas are welcome too; many of RecipeAI's features started as a message from someone cooking with it.
