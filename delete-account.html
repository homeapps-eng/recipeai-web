Delete Your Account

How to delete your RecipeAI account and data

✓ Fastest: delete it in the app

  • Android: open RecipeAI, go to Profile › Settings, tap Remove Account, and confirm.
  • iPhone: open RecipeAI Plus, go to Profile › Settings, tap Delete Account, and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

⚠️ Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium subscription, because Apple and Google Play bill it. Cancel it first:

  • iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › RecipeAI Plus › Cancel Subscription
  • Android: Google Play › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › RecipeAI › Cancel

For refunds, contact Apple or Google Play.

What's deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, right away:

  • your sign-in account, email address, name and profile photo
  • your food preferences, diets, allergies and settings
  • your saved recipes, favorites and history
  • our record of your subscription status

This can't be undone.

What may remain for a while

  • Support emails and content reports you sent us: deleted within 12 months after they're resolved.
  • Server logs, which can include your user ID: kept for about 30 days.
  • Apple and Google keep their own records of your purchases, under their policies.
  • Recipes created from typed or spoken requests may stay in a cache, without any link to you.

Can't use the app? Ask by email

Send a request from the email address of your RecipeAI account:

📧 Email

support@recipe-ai.io

Subject: "Account Deletion Request". We'll delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

📋 Checking it's you

To protect your account, we only delete it when the request comes from the account's email address. If it doesn't, we'll ask you to confirm before deleting anything.

Other privacy requests

You can also ask for a copy of your information, or to correct it, at support@recipe-ai.io. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
